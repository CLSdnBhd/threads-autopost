# Caption style guide

Based on 236 posts from @adiazwari_ (9–20 Nov 2026). The full research is in
`../threads-research/top20-property-threads.html`.

Tone: **BM + English mix (rojak)**, casual, first person ("aku", "kau", "korang").
Write like a friend sharing a cool house, not an agent selling one.

## Main post (with the photo carousel)

Three short lines, at most ~220 characters:

1. **Hook**, using one of the formulas below. Ends with 😂, 🫶🏻, 🤍 or 👀.
2. **One lifestyle benefit**: fully furnished macam hotel, MRT walking distance, KLCC view, big balcony, freehold.
3. **📍 Area, City**

Rules:
- No sqft, no "for sale / for rent", no agent wording ("call now", "limited units", "best deal").
- No phone number or link in the main post. Those go in the reply.
- Price only when the price is the hook (Price surprise formula, or rentals).
- Never claim something that isn't in `details.txt`. If a detail is unclear, ask Calvin.

## Self-reply (posted ~20 s later, carries the lead)

```
[Freehold/Leasehold] | [X] bedrooms [Y] bathrooms [Z] carparks
[Siap by 2029 ✅ / Ready to move in ✅ / Fully furnished ✅]
[Price line: From RM___ / Rental RM___/month]
PM for more details 🫡 or WhatsApp terus 👇
```
The `link` field is a WhatsApp link with a pre-filled message naming the listing:
`https://wa.me/60175495418?text=` + URL-encoded `Hi Calvin, saya nak tahu pasal <short listing name> (Threads)`

## The 7 formulas (rotate, never the same formula twice in a row)

| Formula | Template (rojak) | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Kalau aku kaya | Kalau aku kaya, confirm aku beli rumah [feature] macam ni 🫶🏻 | new launch, premium |
| POV | POV : balik kerja terus rasa macam staycation, fully furnished cantik cam hotel 🤍 | furnished, new launch |
| Serious… | Serious… kalau rumah ada [view/bathtub/big kitchen], weekend memang tak keluar rumah 😂 | one standout feature |
| Price surprise | Aku ingat area [premium area] mesti mahal… rupanya monthly dari RM[x]k je, same as bayar bilik sewa 😂 | **rentals, affordable sale. Best for leads.** |
| Renter comparison | POV : balcony rumah ni sama besar dengan bilik sewa kau 😂 | young buyers, rentals |
| Rumah ni untuk… | Rumah ni untuk yang [bujang / suka masak / WFH / tak suka studio sempit] 🫶🏻 | persona fit, gets tags |
| Live view | Cantik gila view KL malam ni dari balcony rumah ni 😍 | units with a view |

Weekly mix for 14 posts: about 3 Price surprise, 2 each of POV, Kalau aku kaya, Rumah ni untuk… and Renter comparison,
then Serious… and Live view to fill. After the first report, shift the mix toward whatever the report shows wins.

## Rentals vs sale

- **Rentals:** put the monthly rent in the hook (Price surprise or Renter comparison). Reply line: `Rental RM___/month · min 1 year`.
- **New launch:** freehold, completion year, the lifestyle around it (MRT, mall). Price as "From RM___".
- **Subsale:** "Ready to move in ✅", renovation or furnishing, "Fully furnished, bawa baju je" style hooks.

## Photos

- Cover (photo 1) is the "wow" shot: the view from the window or balcony, the living room, or a styled bedroom. Never the lobby, a floor plan or the exterior.
- Order: wow shot → living → bedroom → kitchen → bathroom → facilities → view at night.
- 7–15 photos. Skip blurry, dark, or duplicate angles.
- The same listing can return after 10+ days with a different formula and a different cover.
